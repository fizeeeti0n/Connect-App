The Problem

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't.

The creator economy is broken. Small creators with loyal, engaged audiences stay invisible — while brands waste budgets on overpriced influencers with fake followers.

😞
Creators are invisible

Thousands of talented creators post consistently, grow genuine audiences, and pour their heart into content — yet never get a single brand inquiry. The algorithm buries them before brands ever see them.

💸
Brands can't find authentic voices

Brands overspend on mega-influencers with disengaged followers. Micro-creators with 5–100K audiences often deliver 3× higher engagement — but brands have no reliable way to find them.

🔒
Payments are a nightmare

Creators get ghosted after completing campaigns. Brands get burned by undelivered content. There's no secure, trust-building layer between creators and brands. Until now.

📊
No data, no proof of value

Creators can't prove their impact. Brands can't measure ROI easily. The whole ecosystem runs on DMs and spreadsheets. There's no professional infrastructure for creator-brand relationships at this scale.
